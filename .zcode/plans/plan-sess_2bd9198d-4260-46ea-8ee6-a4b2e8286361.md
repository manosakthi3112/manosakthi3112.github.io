### How each technique shows up

**Skeuomorphism** — realistic, tactile surfaces via layered shadows (outer drop + inset dark) and multi-stop gradients simulating light:
- CRT monitor (hero), oscilloscope gauge + patch-bay (skills), metal-bezel chip cartridges (projects), trophy medals with ribbons (awards), stamped diploma (education), physical switchboard (contact).
- Glossy top highlights, beveled edges, knurled knobs, tactile toggle switch for the theme.

**Claymorphism** — soft puffy rounded surfaces: large `border-radius`, inset top white highlight + large soft outer shadow + soft inset bottom shadow:
- Floating stat pills (8.8 GPA, 9 hackathon wins, 12 projects, 8 certs), skill pills, holo-panels behind content blocks, the contact card.

**3D Parallax** — every major section is a scene with 3–4 depth tiers (`data-parallax-speed`):
- Layer 0 back: ambient gradient + drifting glow blobs
- Layer 1: structural rails/shelves
- Layer 2: content (mid)
- Layer 3: foreground floating clay particles / highlights
- Moves on **scroll** (GSAP scrub) **and** on **cursor** (rAF-throttled, desktop-only / fine-pointer). Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` and degrades gracefully on touch.
